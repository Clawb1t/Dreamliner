Plus Jakarta Sans (the dreamliner.site typeface), used by the /profile card.

PlusJakartaSans-500/650/750.ttf are static weights cut from the site's variable font
(axis wght 200-800) with fontTools, so the card can match the site's CSS weights exactly.
